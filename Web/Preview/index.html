Jump, then hold Shift and keep holding: webs chain by themselves. Let go while the needle sits in the green band for a perfect release, which builds speed tiers up to 70 m/s. Gamepads work too (RT swing, A jump). On a phone, use the on-screen stick and buttons.

A browser preview of the game's movement code, running the same rules that the Unreal build uses, in placeholder art. No combat, story or final visuals yet.
